Before applying

Basic loan requirements

Applicant details

  • Full name, mobile number, email, city, and complete address.
  • Age 21 years or above, with stable income source.
  • PAN number and Aadhaar number or Aadhaar last four digits.
  • Employment type, monthly income, loan amount, and tenure preference.

Documents to upload

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card or other valid identity proof.
  • Latest salary slips, bank statement, or business income proof.
  • Address proof such as Aadhaar, voter ID, rent agreement, or utility bill.
  • Property, vehicle, or collateral documents when applying for secured loans.

Important terms

Loan approval, interest rate, processing fee, EMI, tenure, and final eligibility depend on lender policy, credit profile, document verification, income proof, and repayment capacity.
